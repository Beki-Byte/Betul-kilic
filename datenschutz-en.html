The “Send feedback” button only opens your own email program. If you write to me, I use your address and message solely to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is deleted once it is no longer needed and is not shared with third parties. Emails are received via Google (Gmail).

05Storage in your browser

Your language choice is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). So that the loading animation does not run at full length again in the same session, your browser remembers this for the session (sessionStorage). This information is not sent to me or anyone else and is only needed for the function you requested. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

06Demo assistant

The demo assistant on the home page runs entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent anywhere and is not stored.

The website contains links to other sites, such as GitHub. When you click a link, the privacy policy of that site applies.
